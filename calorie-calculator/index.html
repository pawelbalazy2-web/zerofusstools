What BMR and TDEE mean

BMR (Basal Metabolic Rate) is the number of calories your body burns at complete rest, just to keep basic functions running. TDEE (Total Daily Energy Expenditure) adds your activity level on top — it's the number closer to your actual daily calorie needs.

How to use the result

Treat TDEE as a starting reference point, not a strict rule. It's a useful baseline for a conversation with a nutritionist or doctor, especially before making significant changes to your diet.

Is this result exact?

It's a statistical estimate — actual needs vary with genetics, body composition, and health status, so real values can differ.

Is my data stored anywhere?

No — all calculations happen locally in your browser.
