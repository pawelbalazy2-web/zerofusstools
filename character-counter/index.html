When this comes in handy

Many platforms have strict length limits: a product description, an SEO meta description (usually 150–160 characters), a tweet, or a school assignment with a minimum word count. Paste your text here and see the exact count in real time instead of guessing.

How reading time is calculated

Based on an average adult reading speed of about 200 words per minute — a useful reference point when planning the length of a blog post or presentation.
