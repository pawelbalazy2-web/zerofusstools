Currency Converter

Enter today's exchange rate (check your bank or a currency site), and this tool converts the amount.

—

This tool doesn't auto-fetch live rates — rates change daily. Check your bank or a currency site and enter it manually above.

Why no automatic rate?

Exchange rates change in real time, and different providers (banks, exchange offices, card networks) apply different margins. Instead of showing a rate that might be stale or wrong for your specific transaction, this tool lets you enter exactly the rate you see from your provider.
