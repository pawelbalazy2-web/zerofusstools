How it's calculated

The discount amount is the original price multiplied by the discount percentage. Subtract that from the original price to get the sale price. For example, 25% off $200 saves you $50, bringing the price to $150.

Can I stack two discounts?

This tool calculates one discount at a time. Stacked discounts don't simply add up (20% + 10% isn't 30%) — apply the second discount to the already-reduced price.
