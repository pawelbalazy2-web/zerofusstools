How the due date is calculated

Naegele's rule assumes pregnancy lasts about 280 days (40 weeks) from the first day of your last period, based on a regular 28-day cycle. It's the standard method for an initial due date estimate, though only a small percentage of babies arrive on the exact calculated day — the actual date usually falls within a two-week window either side.
