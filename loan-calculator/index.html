How the monthly payment is calculated

With an equal-payment loan, you pay the same amount every month for the full term, though the split between principal and interest shifts over time — early payments are mostly interest, later ones mostly principal. This is the standard structure for most personal and auto loans.

What affects your payment

Three factors matter most: the loan amount (higher amount, higher payment), the interest rate (even a small difference compounds significantly over a long term), and the loan term (a longer term lowers the monthly payment but increases total interest paid).
