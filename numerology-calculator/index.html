What the life path number is

The life path number is a core concept in numerology — calculated by summing all the digits of your birth date down to a single digit (except for the "master numbers" 11, 22, and 33, which stay unreduced). It's believed to reflect core personality traits and life direction.

Numerology is symbolic and for entertainment — it's not a scientifically validated method of predicting personality or the future.
