Generate a QR Code

From a link, text, or phone number — ready to download in seconds.

What QR codes are useful for

QR codes are a fast way to move someone from print (a business card, poster, or menu) straight to a webpage — just a camera scan. Handy for sharing a link, phone number, Wi-Fi details, or a payment link.

How this generator works

The QR code is created entirely in your browser — the text you enter is never sent to a server. Download it as PNG and use it anywhere.
