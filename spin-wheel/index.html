Spin the Wheel

Enter your own options, spin the wheel, and see what you get.

What this wheel is good for

A visual, fun way to make a group decision — where to eat, who goes first, which task goes to whom, or picking a giveaway winner on social media. Unlike a plain random number, a spinning wheel adds a bit of drama to the process.

How to use it

Type your options on the right, one per line, click "Update wheel," then "Spin the wheel." The wheel automatically adjusts the number and colors of segments to match your list.
