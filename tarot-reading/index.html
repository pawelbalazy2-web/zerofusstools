What tarot is

Tarot is a 78-card deck used for centuries as a tool for reflection. This tool draws from the 22 Major Arcana — the most recognizable part of the deck, representing core archetypes and stages of human experience, from The Fool (new beginnings) to The World (fulfillment).

Reading the three-card spread

Past-Present-Future is one of the most popular spreads — the first card shows influences from the past, the second your current situation, and the third a possible direction ahead.

Tarot is symbolic and for entertainment — it doesn't predict the future or replace professional advice.
