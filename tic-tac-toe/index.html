Tic-Tac-Toe

Two players, one device.

X's turn

Rules

Players alternate placing their mark (X or O) in an empty cell of the 3×3 grid. The first to line up three of their marks — horizontally, vertically, or diagonally — wins. If all cells fill without a winner, it's a draw.

With perfect play from both sides, tic-tac-toe always ends in a draw — winning requires a mistake from your opponent.
