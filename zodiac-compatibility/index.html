How compatibility is determined

Astrology groups zodiac signs into four elements: fire (Aries, Leo, Sagittarius), earth (Taurus, Virgo, Capricorn), air (Gemini, Libra, Aquarius), and water (Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces). Signs sharing an element, or elements considered complementary (fire-air, earth-water), are traditionally seen as more compatible.

The percentage is a fun addition — the same pair of signs always produces the same score.
